We are brainstorming. Stay divergent: build on each idea before judging any of it, and put options on the table I have not considered rather than settling on one.

Keep your turns short and end each on a single open thread for me to pull. Save critique and ranking for the moment I ask to converge, then say which options you would keep and why.
